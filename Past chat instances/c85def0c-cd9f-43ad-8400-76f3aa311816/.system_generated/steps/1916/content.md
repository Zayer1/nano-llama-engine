<p>The 2024 pattern was a single large model wired to your tools, told to do everything. It mostly worked. Then a better model shipped. Then another. The thing you paid for last summer looks like a toy now.</p>

<ul>

<li>Bad at any one thing in particular.</li>

<h3>The SkillMix way — a team of specialists, each one swappable</h3>

<h2>Anatomy of a swarm — seven roles in conversation</h2>
<p>Not every swarm uses every role. Simple jobs run with three. Complex ones run with fifteen. But almost every swarm we build is composed from this shortlist of archetypes.</p>
<h3>01 · Scout</h3>
<p>Fetches. Searches the web, your knowledge base, the CRM, anywhere the answer might live. Doesn&#39;t judge what it finds; just returns it. <em>Tool-heavy · parallel · cheap model.</em></p>
<h3>02 · Archivist</h3>
<p>Remembers. Maintains the swarm&#39;s shared memory: past decisions, client context, canonical facts. Asked before any new research runs. <em>Long-context · retrieval.</em></p>
<h3>03 · Drafter</h3>
<p>Writes. Proposals, replies, summaries, reports. Uses the Scout&#39;s findings and the Archivist&#39;s memory to produce the first version. <em>Frontier model · style-tuned.</em></p>
<h3>04 · Critic</h3>
<p>Reviews. Fact-checks every claim, stress-tests the logic, flags what doesn&#39;t hold up. The swarm&#39;s skeptic. Never ships anything unreviewed. <em>Different model · lower temp.</em></p>
<h3>05 · Router</h3>
<p>Dispatches. Decides which role handles what, what runs in parallel, what escalates. The swarm&#39;s traffic controller. <em>Small model · fast · deterministic.</em></p>
<h3>06 · Executor</h3>
<p>Acts. Writes to the CRM, sends the email, books the meeting, files the ticket. The only role that touches your real systems. Narrow and audited. <em>Scoped permissions · audited.</em></p>
<h3>07 · Escalator</h3>
<p>Knows when to call a human. Watches for edge cases, confidence drops, policy boundaries. Hands off with full context attached. <em>Rules + judgment · Slack / email.</em></p>
<h3>08 · A role that doesn&#39;t exist yet</h3>
<p>Every serious swarm we&#39;ve built introduced at least one role we hadn&#39;t named before. Yours will too.</p>

<h2>Anatomy of a swarm — seven roles in conversation</h2>
<p>Not every swarm uses every role. Simple jobs run with three. Complex ones run with fifteen. But almost every swarm we build is composed from this shortlist of archetypes.</p>
<h3>01 · Scout</h3>
<p>Fetches. Searches the web, your knowledge base, the CRM, anywhere the answer might live. Doesn&#39;t judge what it finds; just returns it. <em>Tool-heavy · parallel · cheap model.</em></p>
<h3>02 · Archivist</h3>
<p>Remembers. Maintains the swarm&#39;s shared memory: past decisions, client context, canonical facts. Asked before any new research runs. <em>Long-context · retrieval.</em></p>
<h3>03 · Drafter</h3>
<p>Writes. Proposals, replies, summaries, reports. Uses the Scout&#39;s findings and the Archivist&#39;s memory to produce the first version. <em>Frontier model · style-tuned.</em></p>
<h3>04 · Critic</h3>
<p>Reviews. Fact-checks every claim, stress-tests the logic, flags what doesn&#39;t hold up. The swarm&#39;s skeptic. Never ships anything unreviewed. <em>Different model · lower temp.</em></p>
<h3>05 · Router</h3>
<p>Dispatches. Decides which role handles what, what runs in parallel, what escalates. The swarm&#39;s traffic controller. <em>Small model · fast · deterministic.</em></p>
<h3>06 · Executor</h3>
<p>Acts. Writes to the CRM, sends the email, books the meeting, files the ticket. The only role that touches your real systems. Narrow and audited. <em>Scoped permissions · audited.</em></p>
<h3>07 · Escalator</h3>
<p>Knows when to call a human. Watches for edge cases, confidence drops, policy boundaries. Hands off with full context attached. <em>Rules + judgment · Slack / email.</em></p>
<h3>08 · A role that doesn&#39;t exist yet</h3>
<p>Every serious swarm we&#39;ve built introduced at least one role we hadn&#39;t named before. Yours will too.</p>
